> **A Quick-Start Guide for Emerging Leaders**  
> *A concise, accessible guide focusing on core leadership principles and practical action.*

---

# Course Introduction

## Welcome to the Journey

Leadership often starts before you feel ready. 

Someone asks you to help coordinate a team, mentor a person, host a small group, or take on a new responsibility. You might feel excited, nervous, or both.

It is normal to ask: *"What am I supposed to do?"*

Skills matter. You will learn to speak up, listen well, plan ahead, and work with others. But Christian leadership begins with a much bigger question:

> **"Who am I becoming as I follow Jesus and serve others?"**

God cares about what you do, but He cares even more about who you are on the inside. 

You do not need a special title or badge to be a leader. Whenever you help someone, set a good example, or take responsibility, you are leading.

---

## The 4 H's: How a Leader Grows

In every module, you will look at four parts of your life:

| Area | Question | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🧠 **Head** | *What do I need to know?* | Learning God's truth and how to think wisely. |
| ❤️ **Heart** | *Who am I becoming?* | Growing in love, honesty, humility, and patience. |
| ✋ **Hands** | *What do I need to do?* | Putting what you learn into action where you are right now. |
| 🔁 **Habits** | *What will I practice?* | Small daily habits that help you grow over time. |

---

## The 6 Modules at a Glance

1. **God Uses Your Story** — How your past shapes who you are today.
2. **God Forms Your Character** — How hard times grow your patience and trust.
3. **God Builds Your Faithfulness** — Why doing small things well matters so much.
4. **God Develops Your Gifts** — Finding your strengths and using them to help others.
5. **God Shapes How You Lead People** — Leading with love, good listening, and care.
6. **God Deepens Your Life With Him** — Staying close to Jesus so you do not run dry.

---

## How to Prepare Each Week

You do not have to write long essays. This is not an exam or a formal evaluation!

* **Keep it simple**: Use bullet points or single words.
* **Be honest**: Share what is real, not just what sounds good.
* **Talk with God**: Use the simple *Notice, Name, Receive, Respond* prayer steps.
* **Bring 3 things to your group**:
  1. One thing that encouraged you.
  2. One honest question you have.
  3. One small step you tried this week.

# Module 1: God Uses Your Story

> **Big Idea:** God uses your background, your family, and your life experiences to shape how you lead. He can heal past hurts and use your story for good.  
> **Key Bible Passage:** Genesis 50:15–21 (Joseph forgives his brothers)  
> **This Week's Habit:** *Notice and Learn*

> *"You intended to harm me, but God intended it all for good. He brought me to this position so I could save the lives of many people."*  
> — **Genesis 50:20 (NLT)**

---

### 1. Real Life: Two Different Leaders

Think about two new leaders:

* **Leader A** grew up in a home where people talked through problems calmly. When tension happens in her group, she is not afraid to talk about it and help people make peace.
* **Leader B** grew up where arguments were scary and loud. When tension happens in his group, he tries to ignore it or make a joke to avoid talking about it.

Neither leader started from scratch. Both brought their past into how they lead.

Your family background, upbringing, friendships, life milestones, and painful moments have all shaped you. Some experiences give you strengths. Other experiences make you want to protect yourself or hide.

---

### 2. Core Truth

God does not waste any part of your story. 

Think about Joseph in the Bible. His brothers betrayed him and sold him as a slave. He was falsely accused and thrown into prison. Yet through all those hard years, God was shaping him. Later, Joseph became a great leader who saved thousands of people from starvation. 

Joseph could say to his brothers: *"You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good."*

---

### 3. The 4 H's for Module 1

* 🧠 **Head (What to Know):** Your past affects how you act today. When you know your story, you can make better choices as a leader.
* ❤️ **Heart (Who to Become):** A leader who brings past hurts and fears to God instead of taking them out on others.
* ✋ **Hands (What to Do):** Notice when you get upset, defensive, or scared. Ask yourself: *"Why am I reacting this way?"*
* 🔁 **Habit (Notice and Learn):** At the end of each day, take 2 minutes to write down one moment when you felt strong emotions, and ask God what it shows you.

---

### 4. Bring It Before God

Take 5 minutes to pray through these 4 steps:

1. **Notice:** Think of one experience from your past (good or hard) that still affects you today.
2. **Name:** Tell God honestly how that experience made you feel (grateful, angry, afraid, or insecure).
3. **Receive:** Ask God to give you His comfort, forgiveness, and healing truth.
4. **Respond:** Ask God: *"How can You use this part of my story to help someone else?"*

---

### 5. Group Discussion Questions

1. What is one positive experience or person from your past that helped shape who you are today?
2. When something goes wrong or someone disagrees with you, do you usually get angry, pull away, or try to fix it right away?
3. How does knowing that God is in control of your story give you hope?

# Module 2: God Forms Your Character

> **Big Idea:** True leadership is built on who you are when nobody is watching and how you act under pressure.  
> **Key Bible Passage:** Romans 5:1–5 & 1 Samuel 24:1–12 (David spares Saul's life)  
> **This Week's Habit:** *Pause and Pray*

> *"We can rejoice, too, when we run into problems and trials, for we know that they help us develop endurance. And endurance develops strength of character, and character strengthens our confident hope of salvation."*  
> — **Romans 5:3–4 (NLT)**

---

### 1. Real Life: The Pressure Test

Imagine a tea bag. You only find out what is inside when you drop it into boiling hot water.

Leadership is like hot water. When you are tired, busy, criticized, or stressed:
* Insecurity can make you crave everyone’s approval.
* Anger can make you speak harshly.
* Pride can make you blame others when things go wrong.

Pressure does not create what is inside you; it reveals what is already there.

---

### 2. Core Truth

God cares far more about your **character** (who you are) than your **reputation** (what people think of you).

In 1 Samuel 24, David was hiding in a dark cave from King Saul, who was trying to kill him. Saul walked into the very same cave. David’s men whispered, *"This is your chance! Kill him and become king right now!"*

David could have taken the shortcut. But he chose to honor God and spare Saul’s life. David refused to do the wrong thing to get to the right place. He trusted God to promote him in God’s time.

---

### 3. The 4 H's for Module 2

* 🧠 **Head (What to Know):** Hard times and pressure are not mistakes. God uses them like a gym workout to build your spiritual muscles.
* ❤️ **Heart (Who to Become):** A leader who chooses honesty, humility, and patience even when it is difficult.
* ✋ **Hands (What to Do):** When you feel angry or stressed, stop before you send that text message or reply.
* 🔁 **Habit (Pause and Pray):** Whenever you feel tension rising, take 3 slow, deep breaths. Silently pray: *"Lord, give me Your peace and wisdom right now."*

---

### 4. Bring It Before God

1. **Notice:** When was the last time you felt stressed, frustrated, or treated unfairly?
2. **Name:** Tell God honestly what came out of your mouth or heart in that moment.
3. **Receive:** Thank Jesus for His forgiveness and ask the Holy Spirit to fill you with patience and self-control.
4. **Respond:** If you hurt someone or spoke unkindly, make it right by offering a quick, sincere apology.

---

### 5. Group Discussion Questions

1. When you feel stressed or overloaded, what is your first reaction?
2. Why is it tempting to take shortcuts when you want to achieve something good?
3. What is one character quality you really want God to grow in you this month?

# Module 3: God Builds Your Faithfulness

> **Big Idea:** Big leadership starts with small things. If you are faithful with what you have today, God can trust you with more tomorrow.  
> **Key Bible Passage:** Luke 16:10–12 & Matthew 25:14–23 (The Parable of the Talents)  
> **This Week's Habit:** *Write It Down and Follow Through*

> *"If you are faithful in little things, you will be faithful in large ones. But if you are dishonest in little things, you won't be honest with greater responsibilities."*  
> — **Luke 16:10 (NLT)**

---

### 1. Real Life: The Forgotten Job

Someone asks you to pack up equipment after an event or send a message by Wednesday. 

It seems small and boring. No one is cheering for you. So you forget, or you leave it for someone else to finish.

Many people want to do "great things" for God, but few people want to do the small, hidden tasks with a joyful heart. Yet in God's kingdom, the way you treat small duties shows whether you are ready for bigger responsibilities.

---

### 2. Core Truth

God measures leadership by **faithfulness**, not by fame or popularity.

In Jesus’ parable of the talents, the master did not say, *"Well done, my famous and powerful servant."* He said:

> *"Well done, my good and faithful servant. You have been faithful in handling this small amount, so now I will give you many more responsibilities."* (Matthew 25:21 NLT)

Faithfulness means:
* Showing up on time.
* Doing what you promised to do.
* Giving your best effort even when no one is watching.
* Saying *"No"* politely when you are too busy, so you don't break promises.

---

### 3. The 4 H's for Module 3

* 🧠 **Head (What to Know):** Trust is built in drops and lost in buckets. Reliability is one of the greatest gifts you can give your team.
* ❤️ **Heart (Who to Become):** A servant leader who finds joy in honoring God through ordinary, quiet tasks.
* ✋ **Hands (What to Do):** Pick one small job you have been putting off and finish it well today.
* 🔁 **Habit (Write It Down and Follow Through):** Whenever you make a promise or agree to do a task, write it down immediately on paper or in your phone. Check it off when finished.

---

### 4. Bring It Before God

1. **Notice:** What small duties or promises are currently on your plate?
2. **Name:** Confess to God if you have been careless, lazy, or overcommitted lately.
3. **Receive:** Receive God's strength to be diligent and reliable, knowing He sees every quiet act of service.
4. **Respond:** Choose one task you will complete faithfully this week with a cheerful attitude.

---

### 5. Group Discussion Questions

1. Can you think of someone you really trust? What makes them so reliable?
2. Which small, hidden tasks in your life or church do you find hardest to do with a good attitude?
3. How can writing things down or setting phone reminders help you protect your integrity?

# Module 4: God Develops Your Gifts

> **Big Idea:** God has given you unique gifts, talents, and abilities. You don't have to be good at everything, but you are called to serve others with what you have.  
> **Key Bible Passage:** 1 Peter 4:10–11 & Romans 12:3–8  
> **This Week's Habit:** *Serve, Notice, and Ask*

> *"God has given each of you a gift from his great variety of spiritual gifts. Use them well to serve one another."*  
> — **1 Peter 4:10 (NLT)**

---

### 1. Real Life: The Comparison Trap

You look at someone else leading. They speak with ease, make everyone laugh, and never seem nervous. You think: *"I could never do that. I guess I'm not a leader."*

Or maybe the opposite happens: You are really good at something, and you start looking down on others who struggle with it.

Both traps come from comparing yourself to others. God never asked you to be a copy of someone else. He made you to be you.

---

### 2. Core Truth

The Church is like a human body (1 Corinthians 12). An eye cannot do what a foot does, and an ear cannot do what a hand does. Every single part is needed.

God gives gifts for two clear reasons:
1. To help and build up other people.
2. To bring glory to God, not to show off.

How do you discover your gifts? You don't discover them by sitting alone in a room taking quizzes. You discover them by **serving**:
* Try different roles (welcoming, tech, music, speaking, organizing, encouraging).
* Notice what brings joy to others and where God brings good fruit.
* Ask trusted leaders what strengths they see in you.

---

### 3. The 4 H's for Module 4

* 🧠 **Head (What to Know):** You are part of a team. Healthy leadership means celebrating other people’s strengths instead of feeling threatened by them.
* ❤️ **Heart (Who to Become):** Humble and secure, thankful for your gifts and eager to see others succeed.
* ✋ **Hands (What to Do):** Use one of your strengths to help a leader or teammate who has a different gift.
* 🔁 **Habit (Serve, Notice, and Ask):** Ask a mentor or friend: *"Where do you think I am most helpful when I serve?"*

---

### 4. Bring It Before God

1. **Notice:** What activities or ways of serving give you joy and help others?
2. **Name:** Talk to God about any jealousy, comparison, or fear of failure you carry.
3. **Receive:** Thank God for making you uniquely and giving you gifts to serve His people.
4. **Respond:** Offer your specific skills to God today and ask Him to open a door to use them.

---

### 5. Group Discussion Questions

1. What is one thing you enjoy doing that seems to help or encourage others?
2. Have you ever caught yourself comparing your gifts with someone else? How did it make you feel?
3. How can our team make better use of everyone’s different strengths?

# Module 5: God Shapes How You Lead People

> **Big Idea:** Jesus showed us that leading is not about bossing people around. Great leaders serve others, listen carefully, and build trust.  
> **Key Bible Passage:** Mark 10:42–45 & Philippians 2:1–5  
> **This Week's Habit:** *Listen Before You Speak*

> *"Whoever wants to be a leader among you must be your servant, and whoever wants to be first among you must be the slave of everyone. For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve others, and to give his life as a ransom for many."*  
> — **Mark 10:43–45 (NLT)**

---

### 1. Real Life: The Boss vs. The Servant

In the world, people often think being a leader means having power:
* Telling everyone what to do.
* Demanding respect.
* Being the center of attention.

Jesus turned this completely upside down. He said, *"The kings of this world rule over their people, but among you it will be different."* 

Then Jesus got down on His knees with a bowl of water and a towel and washed the dirty feet of His disciples (John 13).

---

### 2. Core Truth

Christian leadership is **servant leadership**.

People don’t follow a title; they follow someone who genuinely cares about them. You earn influence by:
1. **Listening well**: Hearing people's thoughts and feelings before making decisions.
2. **Showing appreciation**: Noticing when people help and saying a sincere *"Thank you!"*
3. **Being approachable**: Being easy to talk to and admitting when you make a mistake.

| World's Idea of Leadership | Jesus' Way of Leadership |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"Serve me because I am at the top."* | *"I will serve you so you can grow."* |
| Demands attention and credit | Gives credit to the team and lifts others up |
| Tells people what to do | Listens, understands, and leads by example |

---

### 3. The 4 H's for Module 5

* 🧠 **Head (What to Know):** True authority comes from love and humility, not from control or loud voices.
* ❤️ **Heart (Who to Become):** A leader who puts the good of the team ahead of personal credit or popularity.
* ✋ **Hands (What to Do):** In your next conversation or meeting, ask two good questions before sharing your own opinion.
* 🔁 **Habit (Listen Before You Speak):** Practice the "3-Second Rule": When someone finishes speaking, pause for 3 seconds before you reply to make sure you truly understood them.

---

### 4. Bring It Before God

1. **Notice:** Who is one person you lead or serve with who is hard to get along with?
2. **Name:** Tell God if you have been impatient, selfish, or critical toward them.
3. **Receive:** Ask Jesus to give you His gentle, servant heart for that person.
4. **Respond:** Do one kind, practical act of service for that person this week without drawing attention to yourself.

---

### 5. Group Discussion Questions

1. Think of a leader who made you feel valued and supported. What did they do?
2. Why is it hard to listen quietly when we think we already know the answer?
3. What is one practical way you can serve your team members this week?

# Module 6: God Deepens Your Life With Him

> **Big Idea:** You cannot give what you do not have. To lead others well for the long haul, you must stay closely connected to Jesus every day.  
> **Key Bible Passage:** John 15:1–8 (The Vine and the Branches)  
> **This Week's Habit:** *Abide and Review*

> *"Yes, I am the vine; you are the branches. Those who remain in me, and I in them, will produce much fruit. For apart from me you can do nothing."*  
> — **John 15:5 (NLT)**

---

### 1. Real Life: The Empty Battery

Think about your smartphone. No matter how expensive, shiny, or powerful it is, if the battery runs down to 0%, it turns off and becomes useless.

Many leaders make the mistake of running on empty. They get so busy *working for God* that they forget to *spend time with God*.

Soon they feel tired, grumpy, cynical, and empty inside. Leadership becomes a heavy burden instead of a joyful privilege.

---

### 2. Core Truth

Jesus said: *"Remain in me, and I will remain in you. A branch cannot produce fruit if it is severed from the vine."*

Your public leadership must flow from your private walk with God.
* **Root before Fruit**: Trees with deep roots survive the hottest summers and strongest storms.
* **Rest is not laziness**: Taking time to rest, pray, and read Scripture is what keeps your spiritual engine running.

When you stay close to Jesus:
* His love replaces your insecurity.
* His wisdom guides your decisions.
* His peace guards your heart under pressure.

---

### 3. The 4 H's for Module 6

* 🧠 **Head (What to Know):** Leadership is a marathon, not a sprint. Sustainable leadership requires regular rest and spiritual refuelling.
* ❤️ **Heart (Who to Become):** A disciple first, and a leader second. Your identity is a loved child of God, not your role.
* ✋ **Hands (What to Do):** Set aside 15 minutes of quiet time each day with God, opening your Bible and listening in prayer.
* 🔁 **Habit (Abide and Review):** Take 10 minutes every Sunday evening to look back over your week with God: *Where was God at work? What do I need to surrender for next week?*

---

### 4. Bring It Before God

1. **Notice:** How full is your spiritual battery right now (1% to 100%)?
2. **Name:** Speak openly to God about your tiredness, busyness, or any worries on your mind.
3. **Receive:** Hear Jesus saying to you: *"Come to me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens, and I will give you rest."* (Matthew 11:28 NLT)
4. **Respond:** Choose one time slot in your daily routine where you will consistently meet with God this week.

---

### 5. Group Discussion Questions

1. What usually distracts you or crowds out your personal time with God?
2. What helps you feel most connected and refreshed in your relationship with Jesus?
3. As we finish these 6 modules, what is the single biggest lesson God has taught you about leadership?
