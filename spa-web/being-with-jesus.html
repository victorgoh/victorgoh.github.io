Abide

Being With Jesus Before Doing for Jesus

"I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in Me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from Me you can do nothing." — John 15:5

Welcome, friends! In the busyness of life and serving, it is so easy to find ourselves doing things for God while slowly missing out on time with God. Jesus beautifully reminds us that true fruitfulness doesn't come from striving or performing—it flows from simply abiding in Him.

Whether you are feeling spiritually vibrant, deeply weary, or somewhere in between, Jesus gently invites you to slow down and rest in His presence. Take a few quiet moments to walk through this short self-discovery reflection. There are no right or wrong answers—just an open space to meet with Him.
