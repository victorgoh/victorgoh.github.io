14-DAY PRAYER BOOKLET (SET 1)
Themes: To Pray • To Give • To Serve • To Disciple • To Go

DAY 1 — To Pray: Returning to God
Bible Verse:
“Return to me with all your heart.” — Joel 2:12
Reflection 
The new year begins with an invitation to return to God. As we fast, distractions fade and our hearts become clearer. Returning is not about shame but about restoring closeness. God welcomes every step back with mercy, renewal, and joy. Today, let this journey begin with an honest, simple turning of your heart toward Him again.
Guided Prayer 
“Lord, I return to You today with an open heart. Draw me close and remove whatever has formed distance between us. Renew my desire for You and awaken my spirit to Your presence. Lead me into these days with humility, hunger, and trust in Your love. I surrender this new year to You. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Ask God to soften your heart and renew a hunger for His presence.
Surrender anything that has distracted you spiritually.
Invite the Holy Spirit to reveal what He wants to restore.


Listening to God
“Lord, what are You inviting me to return to this year?”
“Where does my heart need renewal?”
“What step of obedience or surrender are You revealing?”


Being With God
Take a slow breath and settle into silence.
Pray: “Jesus, I return to You.”
Remain still for a moment, letting His peace rest on you before you move on.
Daily Blessing
“May the Lord draw you close today, renew your heart, and fill you with peace as you return to Him.”


DAY 2 — To Pray: Hearing God’s Voice
Bible Verse:
“My sheep listen to my voice; I know them, and they follow me.” — John 10:27
Reflection 
God desires to speak more often than we expect. His voice comes with peace, clarity, and conviction. Fasting quiets our inner world so we can hear Him more clearly. As you slow down today, trust that the Shepherd knows how to communicate with His sheep. Tune your heart to His guidance and expect Him to lead you gently and faithfully.
Guided Prayer 
“Lord Jesus, open my heart and ears to recognise Your voice today. Silence distractions and help me discern Your leading with clarity and peace. Teach me to follow Your gentle nudges and trust Your wisdom. Let this day deepen my confidence in hearing You more personally and consistently. Lead me step by step. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Ask God to clear inner noise and distractions.
Pray for discernment for important decisions in 2026.
Ask the Spirit to train your heart to recognise God’s voice.


Listening to God
“Lord, what are You speaking to me today?”
“Where are You leading me to trust You more deeply?”
“What truth do I need to hold onto?”


Being With God
Settle into stillness and breathe in slowly.
Pray: “Speak, Lord. I am listening.”
Remain quiet, allowing His presence to calm your heart.
Daily Blessing
“May the Lord open your ears to His voice and guide your steps in peace.”

DAY 3 — To Pray: Aligning With God’s Will
Bible Verse:
“Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven.” — Matthew 6:10
Reflection 
Prayer aligns our desires with God’s heart. Jesus teaches us to seek the Father’s will above our own, trusting that His plans are wise and full of love. As you fast, invite God to reshape your priorities and decisions. Alignment allows you to walk confidently, knowing you’re stepping into His purposes for 2026.
Guided Prayer 
“Father, align my heart with Your will today. Remove any desire that pulls me from Your path and shape my thoughts according to Your wisdom. Give me clarity to recognise Your direction and courage to obey it. Let this year be marked by choices that honour You. Lead my steps into Your purposes. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Surrender your plans for 2026 to God’s leadership.
Pray for desires shaped by God’s character.
Ask for joy in obeying God’s will.


Listening to God
“Lord, what part of my will needs surrender?”
“Where are You inviting me to trust Your direction?”
“What act of obedience are You highlighting?”


Being With God
Take a deep breath and open your hands as a sign of surrender.
Pray: “Your will be done in me.”
Sit quietly, trusting Him to lead you gently.
Daily Blessing
“May God guide your desires and establish His will in your life today.”

DAY 4 — To Give: Receiving God’s Generosity First
Bible Verse:
“Every good and perfect gift is from above…” — James 1:17
Reflection 
Generosity begins with receiving. When we recognise God as the Giver of every good gift, our hearts overflow with gratitude. Fasting slows us down long enough to appreciate His kindness. Today, reflect on how God has provided, protected, and blessed you. Gratitude softens the heart and prepares us to give freely and joyfully.
Guided Prayer 
“Lord, thank You for every gift You’ve placed in my life. Remind me of Your goodness and open my eyes to see Your blessings clearly. Let gratitude shape my attitude and actions. Teach me to reflect Your generosity in the way I give and live today. I offer my heart with thankfulness. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Thank God for His provision and care.
Ask for a heart sensitive to others’ needs.
Pray for joy in giving.


Listening to God
“Lord, what blessings are You reminding me of?”
“Who needs generosity from me this week?”
“Where are You calling me to give freely?”


Being With God
Take a slow breath and reflect on God’s goodness this year.
Pray: “Thank You, Lord, for Your gifts.”
Remain still, allowing gratitude to fill your heart.
Daily Blessing
“May gratitude overflow from your heart and generosity flow from your life.”

DAY 5 — To Give: Living With Open Hands
Bible Verse:
“You will be enriched in every way so that you can be generous on every occasion…”
— 2 Corinthians 9:11
Reflection 
Living with open hands means trusting God as our provider. We release the need to control and instead embrace freedom, generosity, and faith. Fasting loosens our grip on what we hold too tightly. Today, ask God to teach you the joy of giving without fear, trusting that He enriches us so we can enrich others.
Guided Prayer 
“Lord, teach me to live with open hands today. Replace fear with trust and help me see giving as an act of worship. Free me from anything that makes me hold on too tightly. Let my generosity reflect Your heart and bring encouragement to someone who needs it. Guide my steps in kindness. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Ask God to break fear of lack.
Pray for opportunities to give joyfully.
Ask for trust in God’s provision.


Listening to God
“Lord, what am I clinging to too tightly?”
“Where do You want me to trust You more deeply?”
“Who needs generosity from me today?”


Being With God
Pause and notice any tension in your heart or mind.
Pray: “I open my hands to You, Lord.”
Sit quietly, letting trust replace worry.
Daily Blessing
“May your hands be open and your heart filled with God’s generosity.”

DAY 6 — To Give: Offering Yourself to God
Bible Verse:
“Offer your bodies as a living sacrifice…” — Romans 12:1
Reflection 
God desires more than our resources—He desires our whole selves. Offering ourselves to Him is an act of worship. Fasting reminds us that our lives, abilities, and decisions belong to God. Today, offer your time, skills, habits, and plans to Him anew. A surrendered life becomes a powerful instrument in God’s hands.
Guided Prayer 
“Lord, I offer myself to You today—my heart, abilities, habits, and decisions. Shape my life into something that brings You joy. Help me surrender my daily routines and long-term plans. Use me in ways that honour You and bless others. I place my whole life on Your altar with trust and gratitude. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Offer your daily life to God.
Offer your skills and abilities to His service.
Ask for a life marked by worship.


Listening to God
“Lord, what area of my life needs fresh surrender?”
“Where do You want to use me?”
“How can I honour You in my routines?”


Being With God
Slow your breathing and place your hands over your heart.
Pray: “Here I am, Lord.”
Remain quiet, offering yourself to God in stillness.
Daily Blessing
“May your life be a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God.”

DAY 7 — To Serve: Following Jesus’ Humility
Bible Verse:
“For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve…” — Mark 10:45
Reflection 
Service begins with humility—the willingness to place others before ourselves. Jesus modelled a life of compassion, sacrifice, and quiet strength. As you fast today, ask God to form a servant-hearted spirit within you. Serving doesn’t require grand actions; it begins with small, unseen moments where love becomes visible.
Guided Prayer 
“Jesus, make my heart like Yours. Teach me humility that serves without seeking recognition. Help me see others through Your compassionate eyes. Show me practical ways to bless someone today. Let my actions reflect Your character and bring encouragement to those around me. Shape my heart into a servant’s heart. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Pray for humility.
Ask whom you can serve today.
Pray to serve without recognition.


Listening to God
“Lord, whom do You want me to serve today?”
“Where do I need more humility?”
“What small act of service can I offer?”


Being With God
Take a moment to breathe slowly and release your need to be seen.
Pray: “Make me more like You, Jesus.”
Rest in His gentle humility.
Daily Blessing
“May the humility of Christ shape your words, actions, and heart today.”

Let my actions reflect Your heart and bring comfort to someone who feels unseen today. Teach me to love through simple actions. Amen.”
Prayer Points

Ask for strength to serve joyfully.
Pray to notice opportunities to love others in action.


Listening to God
“Who needs loving action from me today?”
“What simple act of kindness can I offer?”
“How can I bring Your love into my relationships?”


Being With God
Pause and let your heart soften toward people in your life.
Pray: “Fill my heart with Your love.”
Sit quietly, letting compassion grow within you.
Daily Blessing
“May love overflow from your life through humble, gentle actions.”

Today, ask God to open your eyes to opportunities for love, witness, and compassion. The harvest is not something we create—it’s something we join. Lift your eyes and see where God is moving.
Guided Prayer 
“Lord, open my eyes to the people You’ve placed around me. Show me where You are already at work. Give me compassion for the hurting and courage to respond with love. Help me recognise divine opportunities and step into them faithfully. Lead me into the harvest with Your heart and wisdom today. Amen.”
Prayer Points
Pray for people far from God.
Ask for open doors for conversations.
Pray for courage for small steps of faith.


Listening to God
“Lord, who around me is open to Your love?”
“Where are You already working?”
“What step of faith can I take today?”


Being With God

Pray: “Open my eyes, Lord.”
Sit quietly, letting His burden become yours.
Daily Blessing
“May God open your eyes to His work and your heart to His mission.”

Today, embrace your role as His ambassador. Wherever you go, He goes with you. Step into the new year assured that you are sent with purpose.

Prayer Points

Pray for opportunities to share Christ’s love.


Listening to God

“What mission are You giving me?”
“Who do You want me to love intentionally?”
